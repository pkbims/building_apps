# Archived: Toronto student rentals (2026-09-18)

Stage 1 research for an iOS app helping students / first-time renters find a place in
Toronto. Dropped by decision before Stage 2; kept because the evidence is reusable.

- `research.md` — the Stage 1 write-up (nine existing solutions, verbatim complaints with
  links, three new ideas, recommended pick).
- `reviewer/` — the html-worker review page (`PORT=7778 python3 server.py`), plus the raw
  review pulls it was built from: `play.json` (Google Play CA, 300 recent reviews per app),
  `reviews.json` (App Store CA).

Why dropped: user decision, not a research finding.
