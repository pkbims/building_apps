## Source notes

**Worked**
- TrustMRR: `/category/fintech` (458 startups), `/category/ai`, `/category/saas`, `/category/productivity`, `/category/crypto-web3` (77 startups) all 200. **Best trick: `https://trustmrr.com/startup/<slug>.md`** returns clean markdown with verified 30d/12mo/all-time revenue, MRR, growth, pricing, founded date. `https://trustmrr.com/api/ai/discovery` gives 25 recent + 25 fastest-growing (no finance hits except AltIndex). Category pages only render the first 30 startups (`?page=2` returns the same 30); WebSearch `site:trustmrr.com <term>` was the way to find more slugs.
- HN Algolia Show HN API with `numericFilters=created_at_i%3E1759276800` worked via curl. Finance Show HNs mostly get 1–10 points; the standouts were TrackSuccession (191), LangAlpha (148), Write It Down personal-finance sheet (271, 2025-10-06 — general, not couples), Primoco budget app (159, 2026-01-13 — general).
- App Store: `itunes.apple.com/search` and `/lookup` APIs (curl) give rating counts + release dates — very useful proxy for traction of new apps.
- Google Play: curl of the details page and regex for "Downloads" / reviews worked (WebFetch returned empty).
- Product Hunt: product pages (/products/<slug>) worked with WebFetch and returned upvotes, launch date, followers.

**Failed / 404 / limits**
- TrustMRR `/category/finance` → 404; `/category/crypto` → 404 (real slug is `/category/crypto-web3`). `/startup/best-couples-app` (shown in search as "$148,836 last 30 days", a relationship app, not finance) → 404 now. Slug guesses `tradehunterr`, `chartlens`, `chartview` → 404. TrustMRR `/search?q=` returns no server-rendered results.
- Product Hunt monthly leaderboards 2026/7, 2026/8, 2026/9: WebFetch only showed the top 10 each month, and **none were finance/investing/crypto** (all AI agents/dev tools). PH topic page `/topics/investing` returned generic giants (Notion, Stripe), not useful.
- Google Play pages via WebFetch: empty/truncated (use curl).
- calmspend.com: TLS cert expired (couldn't verify).
- Reddit skipped per instructions. No browser tools used.

**Observations for idea-picking (not tools)**
- Couples: big incumbents (Honeydue, Splitwise, Buddy) are old; Zeta died (May 2025) and Tandem got bought by a credit union (Nov 2025). Dozens of new indie couples apps launched 2025–26, but almost none have >15 ratings, and the one TrustMRR couples-budget app (Kontu) has $0. Demand is clear; nobody new has broken out.
- Stocks: the verified money is in **narrow, pro-ish signals for active traders** (alt-data scores, options flow, dilution, Trump alerts, AI second opinion): $1k–$17k MRR at $30–$300/mo price points. AI "upload a chart" apps pull thousands of App Store ratings.
- Crypto: verified indie revenue clusters around **prediction-market (Polymarket) tracking/alerts** and **Hyperliquid perp orderflow**, not classic portfolio trackers (owned by CoinStats/CoinTracker/Koinly). Expect spikes and collapses (Oddpool -97%).
